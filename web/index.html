Control the slingshot with index finger gestures in real time!

Webcam Activation Required

This game uses your webcam and MediaPipe to detect hand gestures. No video is sent to any server; all tracking happens entirely on your local GPU.

How to Play

  • Aim Slingshot: Move your index finger in the screen view. Keep your other fingers closed (isolate the index finger).
  • Arm / Load Bird: Scroll/slide index finger horizontally in SELECTION mode, then Pinch or Z-Push to lock.
  • Launch Bird: Aim and drag your index finger, then move it close to the window boundaries or release to launch.
  • Restart: Press R to restart.
  • Levels: Press 1, 2, or 3 to switch levels.
